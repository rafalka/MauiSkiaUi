# Getting help

- **Documentation:** start with the [README](README.md), the [control pages](docs/controls/README.md), and the [migration guide](docs/Migration.md) for moving MAUI pages to SkiaUi.
- **Questions and ideas:** ask in [Discussions](https://github.com/rafalka/MauiSkiaUi/discussions). Search there first; someone may have asked already.
- **Bugs:** open a [bug report](https://github.com/rafalka/MauiSkiaUi/issues/new?template=bug_report.yml) with a minimal reproduction.
- **Feature requests:** open a [feature request](https://github.com/rafalka/MauiSkiaUi/issues/new?template=feature_request.yml).
- **Security problems:** report them privately, as [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md) describes.

SkiaUi is maintained in spare time, without a support contract. Answers may take a few days.
